About

Built For Builders.

Cyna Model Works is a dedicated scale modelling service focused on transforming plastic model kits into clean, detailed and display-ready pieces. Every project is approached with the mindset of a modeller — not simply an assembly service.

Services cover Gunpla, humanoid model kits, third-party and bootleg kits, as well as vehicles. Workshops are also available for hobbyists who want to learn the proper techniques for building, sanding, painting, detailing and finishing their own models.
