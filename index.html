ABOUT THE BUILDER

MORE THAN JUST SNAP & BUILD.

Cyna Model Works provides custom assembly, painting, weathering and finishing services for scale model enthusiasts. From clean Gunpla builds to heavily customized machines, every project is treated as a miniature engineering and art piece. I also conduct hands-on model kit workshops for people who want to learn the hobby themselves — from basic assembly and nub removal to sanding, painting, detailing and finishing.
