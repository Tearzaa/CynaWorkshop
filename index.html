ABOUT THE BUILDER

MORE THAN JUST SNAP & BUILD.

Cyna Model Works is a dedicated model kit building service focused on transforming plastic kits into clean, detailed and personalised display pieces.

From Gunpla and humanoid mecha to vehicles and third-party kits, every project is approached with the mindset of a modeller: take your time, respect the details and make every part count.

I also provide hands-on model kit workshops for people who want to learn the hobby themselves. Instead of simply giving you a finished model, the workshop teaches you how to build one.
