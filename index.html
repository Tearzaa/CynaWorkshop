ABOUT THE BUILDER

From Plastic Parts to Display Pieces.

I specialise in custom assembly, painting, weathering, detailing and finishing for Gundam, humanoid mecha, third-party kits and scale vehicles.

Every build is treated as its own project. From careful nub removal and seam cleanup to advanced modifications, decals, lighting and custom paintwork, the goal is simple: turn your kit into a model you will be proud to display.

I also conduct hands-on model kit workshops for beginners and hobbyists who want to learn how to build, sand, paint and improve their own kits.
